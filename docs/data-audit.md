## A1. A broken or blocked dock still offers an open slot

**What the solver does:** H6 treats the slot as unavailable, so any booking on it is re-planned (the guide's dock-breakdown case).

Query: [`db/audit/a01_broken_dock_open_slots.sql`](../db/audit/a01_broken_dock_open_slots.sql)

```sql
SELECT e.dock_event_id, e.event_type, sl.dock_id, sl.slot_id, sl.slot_start_ts, sl.slot_end_ts,
       a.shipment_id AS booked_shipment, a.appointment_status
FROM dock_status_events e
JOIN appointment_slots sl ON sl.dock_id = e.dock_id
LEFT JOIN appointments a
       ON a.slot_id = sl.slot_id AND a.is_current = 1
      AND a.appointment_status IN ('PENDING_CONFIRMATION','CONFIRMED','IN_PROGRESS')
WHERE e.event_type IN ('BREAKDOWN','MAINTENANCE','MANUAL_BLOCK')
  AND sl.slot_status = 'OPEN'
  AND sl.slot_start_ts < COALESCE(e.event_end_ts, 'infinity'::timestamptz)
  AND sl.slot_end_ts > e.event_start_ts
ORDER BY sl.dock_id, sl.slot_start_ts;
```

## A5. No open slot starts after the facility's last allowed start time

**What the solver does:** The slot data already respects LAST_NEW_START_TIME (a start exactly at the limit is allowed); H11 still enforces it when trucks are re-planned. Zero rows is the expected, compliant result.

Query: [`db/audit/a05_slots_after_last_start.sql`](../db/audit/a05_slots_after_last_start.sql)

```sql
SELECT sl.slot_id, sl.dock_id, sl.slot_start_ts, r.rule_id, r.rule_value AS last_new_start
FROM appointment_slots sl
JOIN facilities f ON f.facility_id = sl.facility_id
JOIN facility_rules r
  ON r.facility_id = sl.facility_id
 AND r.rule_type = 'LAST_NEW_START_TIME'
 AND r.active_flag = 1
WHERE sl.slot_status = 'OPEN'
  AND (sl.slot_start_ts AT TIME ZONE f.timezone)::time > r.rule_value::time
ORDER BY sl.slot_id;
```

## A6. Slot coverage differs from facility opening hours

**What the solver does:** The solver plans only on slots that exist; the hours without slots go to the warehouse planner to decide.

Query: [`db/audit/a06_slot_coverage_vs_hours.sql`](../db/audit/a06_slot_coverage_vs_hours.sql)

```sql
SELECT f.facility_id, f.open_time, f.close_time,
       min((sl.slot_start_ts AT TIME ZONE f.timezone)::time) AS first_slot_start,
       max((sl.slot_end_ts AT TIME ZONE f.timezone)::time) AS last_slot_end,
       count(*) AS slots
FROM facilities f
JOIN appointment_slots sl ON sl.facility_id = f.facility_id
GROUP BY f.facility_id, f.open_time, f.close_time
HAVING min((sl.slot_start_ts AT TIME ZONE f.timezone)::time) <> f.open_time
    OR max((sl.slot_end_ts AT TIME ZONE f.timezone)::time) <> f.close_time
ORDER BY f.facility_id;
```
